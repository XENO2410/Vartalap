# Fixed Deposits — Product Note

## Product Variants
- **Regular FD:** interest paid monthly / quarterly / on maturity.
- **Reinvestment FD:** compounded quarterly, single maturity payout.
- **Tax Saver FD:** 5-year lock-in, deduction under Section 80C up to ₹1.5L.
- **Senior Citizen FD:** additional 0.50% over the applicable card rate.
- **NRE / NRO FD:** available to NRIs subject to source-of-fund controls.

## Booking Channels
- Internet banking (retail).
- Mobile app.
- Any branch during business hours.
- Relationship manager for HNI customers.

## Premature Withdrawal
- Allowed on all variants except the 5-year Tax Saver FD.
- Penalty: 1.00% below the applicable card rate for the period run OR the
  booking rate, whichever is lower.
- Sweep-in FDs are broken in FIFO order to fund the linked savings account.

## Nomination
Nomination is mandatory. It can be captured at booking or updated later via
the branch or internet banking. Joint holdings must specify the operating
mandate (either or survivor / former or survivor / joint).

## Auto Renewal
Auto renewal defaults to `Reinvestment` at the prevailing rate for the same
tenure. Customers can switch this off during booking.

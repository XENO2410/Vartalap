# Mutual Funds — Advisor Playbook

## Product Suite
Axis Wealth offers execution-only and advisory access across the following
mutual fund categories:
- Equity: large-cap, flexi-cap, mid-cap, small-cap, ELSS, sector/thematic.
- Debt: liquid, ultra-short, money market, corporate bond, gilt, credit risk.
- Hybrid: aggressive, conservative, balanced advantage, multi-asset.
- International feeder funds (currency hedged and unhedged).

## Suitability Framework
An advisor must complete the customer's risk profile before recommending any
scheme:
1. Risk tolerance questionnaire (12 questions).
2. Time horizon and liquidity check.
3. KYC + FATCA declaration.
Recommendations must map to the resulting risk band (Conservative / Moderate
/ Aggressive). Deviations require written justification and a customer
consent letter.

## SIP Rules
- Minimum SIP: ₹500/month (except direct-plan schemes; check factsheet).
- SIP registration cut-off: 8 days before first debit date.
- Pause / cancel SIP: T-2 working days before the debit date.
- Skipping instalments is allowed up to 3 months in a rolling year without
  breaking the mandate.

## Exit Load Ready-Reckoner
- Equity funds: 1% if redeemed within 12 months of allocation, nil after.
- Liquid funds: graded exit load from day 1 to day 7 as per SEBI norms.
- ELSS: statutory 3-year lock-in — no early exit permitted.

## Advisor Do & Don'ts
Do: disclose commission, provide TER details, share KIM before purchase.
Don't: guarantee returns, make comparisons with fixed deposits without a
disclaimer, or process transactions without a signed suitability report.

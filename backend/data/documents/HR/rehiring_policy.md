# Re-Hiring Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines the criteria under which former employees ("alumni") may be
considered for rehire into Axis Bank.

## Eligibility
An alumnus may be considered for re-hire if all of the following are true:
1. The employee resigned or superannuated from Axis Bank in good standing.
2. There was no adverse audit or disciplinary finding at the time of exit.
3. A cooling period of at least 12 months has elapsed since the exit date.
4. The role being considered is at or below the last held grade at the bank.

## Non-Eligibility
An alumnus is NOT eligible for re-hire if any of the following are true:
- Exit was due to a "termination for cause" or breach of code of conduct.
- The employee is currently under a signed non-solicitation clause with a
  competitor and the clause is unexpired.
- Alumnus was identified as a "Do Not Rehire" during exit interviews.

## Approval Workflow
Every re-hire must be signed off by:
- HR Business Partner for the hiring unit.
- Business head at N-1 to CEO.
- Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO).

## Compensation
- Compensation for rehires is decided based on the target role and market band.
- Prior tenure is counted only for:
  - Gratuity computation.
  - Long-service awards.
- Prior tenure does NOT confer any accrued privileged leave or accumulated PF.

## Exceptions
Exceptions must be endorsed by the CEO and are limited to strategic hires
(business heads, technology leadership, key regulatory roles).

# Performance Appraisal Cycle

## Cycle Timeline
| Phase | Window | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Goal setting | 1 Apr – 30 Apr | Employee + reporting manager |
| Mid-year review | 1 Sep – 30 Sep | Employee + reporting manager |
| Self-appraisal | 1 Mar – 15 Mar | Employee |
| Manager appraisal | 16 Mar – 31 Mar | Reporting manager |
| Normalisation | 1 Apr – 15 Apr | Business-unit HRBP |
| Final letters | 20 Apr – 30 Apr | HR Operations |

## Rating Scale
| Rating | Label | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Outstanding | Exceptional, sustained impact well beyond role expectations. |
| 4 | Exceeds | Consistently exceeds expectations across most goals. |
| 3 | Meets | Meets all critical expectations. |
| 2 | Partially Meets | Meets some but not all critical expectations. |
| 1 | Below | Below expected performance; performance-improvement plan triggered. |

## Normalisation
- Ratings are normalised at the business-unit level with recommended distribution:
  Outstanding <= 8 %, Exceeds <= 25 %, Meets 55 – 65 %, Partially Meets <= 8 %,
  Below <= 4 %.
- Deviations require CHRO sign-off.

## PIP (Performance Improvement Plan)
- Employees rated `Below` are placed on a 90-day PIP.
- HRBP reviews progress every 30 days.
- Continued rating of `Below` after PIP leads to separation.

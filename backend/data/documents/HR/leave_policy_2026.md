# Leave Policy — 2026

## Applicability
This policy applies to all permanent employees of Axis Bank across corporate offices,
branches and contact centres. Contractual and third-party staff are governed by the
terms of their engagement contracts.

## Types of Leave

### Privileged Leave (PL)
- 24 working days per calendar year, credited quarterly (6 per quarter).
- Can be availed up to 3 times a year.
- Minimum block of 3 continuous days per instance.
- Requests must be raised on the HR self-service portal at least 7 working days in
  advance for domestic travel and 14 working days for international travel.
- Unused PL carries forward up to a cap of 60 days.

### Casual Leave (CL)
- 8 working days per calendar year, credited on 1st January.
- Cannot be carried forward.
- Maximum block of 2 continuous days.
- Requires reporting manager approval; can be applied on the same day for genuine
  emergencies.

### Sick Leave (SL)
- 12 working days per calendar year, credited on 1st January.
- Medical certificate mandatory for any block of 3 or more continuous days.
- Cannot be carried forward.

### Maternity Leave
- 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children.
- 12 weeks of paid leave from the third child onwards.
- Adoption leave of 12 weeks for children below 3 months of age.
- Requests must be raised at least 8 weeks before the expected date, with the
  medical certificate attached.

### Paternity Leave
- 10 working days of paid leave available within 90 days of the child's birth.
- Can be split into 2 blocks.

### Bereavement Leave
- 5 working days for immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
- 2 working days for extended family.

## Compensatory Off
- Employees working on declared holidays are eligible for compensatory off within 45 days.
- Compensatory off cannot be encashed.

## Encashment
- On separation, up to 60 days of PL balance is encashed at basic + DA.
- CL and SL are not encashable.

## Escalation
For any dispute regarding leave balance, raise a ticket on HResponse under
`HR > Leave Balance`. First-level SLA is 2 business days.

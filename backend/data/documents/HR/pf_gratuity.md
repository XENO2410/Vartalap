# Provident Fund (PF) & Gratuity — Quick Reference

## Provident Fund
- Contribution: **12% of basic + DA** by the employee, matched by the employer.
- Employer contribution split: 8.33% to EPS (Employee Pension Scheme, capped
  on ₹15,000 basic) + balance to EPF.
- Interest is credited annually as declared by the EPFO.
- Withdrawals: partial withdrawals allowed for defined reasons (marriage,
  higher education, home purchase, medical emergency) subject to service
  tenure thresholds.
- Universal Account Number (UAN) is issued at joining and is portable across
  employers.

## Gratuity
- Payable on separation after continuous service of at least **5 years**
  (waived for death / permanent disability).
- Formula: `(Last drawn basic + DA) × 15 / 26 × completed years of service`.
- Statutory cap: ₹20,00,000 (tax-exempt limit).
- Paid within 30 days of the last working day; delays attract simple interest
  at the notified rate.

## Nomination
Employees must nominate their PF and gratuity beneficiaries within 30 days of
joining and update nominations after any life event (marriage, birth of
child, divorce).

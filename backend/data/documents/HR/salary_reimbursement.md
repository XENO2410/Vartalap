# Salary Reimbursement Policy

## Coverage
This policy covers reimbursement of pre-approved business expenses paid out of
the employee's own pocket. Categories include local conveyance, client
entertainment, mobile / broadband, professional membership fees and business
travel per diem.

## Submission Window
- Claims must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of the expense date.
- Claims older than 60 days are auto-rejected by the system.
- Claims raised between 30 and 60 days require reporting manager + HRBP
  approval.

## Required Documentation
- Original receipt or GST-compliant invoice.
- Purpose note describing the business reason.
- For entertainment claims: list of attendees and their designation.
- For travel: boarding pass copies for flights, e-tickets for train and
  approved travel request ID.

## Category Caps (INR, monthly)
| Grade | Local conveyance | Client entertainment | Mobile / broadband |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M1 | 3,000 | 5,000 | 1,000 |
| M2 | 5,000 | 10,000 | 1,500 |
| M3 | 8,000 | 20,000 | 2,000 |
| M4+ | 12,000 | Actuals | 2,500 |

## Approval Flow
Employee → Reporting Manager → HRBP → Finance Payments.
Payouts credit the salary account within 5 business days of Finance approval.

## Escalation
For payout delays beyond SLA, log a Unidesk ticket under
`HR > Reimbursement > Payout Delay`.

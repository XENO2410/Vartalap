# Advisory: High-value cash deposit

## Situation

A High-value cash deposit situation has been reported or detected.

## Key Considerations

Large cash credits must be reconciled with the declared business profile and escalated where inconsistent.

## Immediate Steps

1. Preserve all evidence in its current state.

2. Inform the owning team and the compliance or legal desk.

3. Do not communicate externally without approval.

## Escalation

Escalate to the relevant officer within the same business day.

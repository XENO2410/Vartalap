# Data Privacy — DPDP Act 2023 Alignment

## What the DPDP Act Requires
The Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 defines how personal
data of Indian citizens ("Data Principals") must be processed by any entity
("Data Fiduciary"). Axis Bank is a Significant Data Fiduciary and follows the
obligations below.

## Lawful Grounds for Processing
Bank processes personal data only on one of the following grounds:
- Explicit consent obtained through the customer journey.
- Legitimate use for the performance of a service the customer has requested.
- Compliance with applicable laws (RBI Master Directions, PMLA, income tax
  reporting).
- Employment obligations for staff data.

## Data Principal Rights
Customers can raise these requests through the customer portal or by writing
to `dpo@axisbank.com`:
- Right to information about processing.
- Right to correction of inaccurate data.
- Right to erasure (subject to statutory retention).
- Right to grievance redressal (30 day SLA).
- Right to nominate a person to exercise their rights.

## Retention
Customer KYC and transaction data are retained for a **minimum of 8 years**
after account closure per PMLA. Beyond that, data is anonymised or purged
unless a legal hold is in effect.

## Data Breach Handling
Any suspected breach must be reported to the DPO within 24 hours by raising
a `Priority-Zero` incident on Unidesk. The DPO reports notifiable breaches to
the Data Protection Board within 72 hours.

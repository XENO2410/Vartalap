# Advisory: Data subject request

## Situation

A Data subject request situation has been reported or detected.

## Key Considerations

Access and erasure requests carry statutory timelines and need the privacy desk's coordination.

## Immediate Steps

1. Preserve all evidence in its current state.

2. Inform the owning team and the compliance or legal desk.

3. Do not communicate externally without approval.

## Escalation

Escalate to the relevant officer within the same business day.

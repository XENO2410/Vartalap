# Advisory: Cheque dishonour dispute

## Situation

A Cheque dishonour dispute situation has been reported or detected.

## Key Considerations

Dishonour handling must follow the NI Act process, including return memos and customer communication.

## Immediate Steps

1. Preserve all evidence in its current state.

2. Inform the owning team and the compliance or legal desk.

3. Do not communicate externally without approval.

## Escalation

Escalate to the relevant officer within the same business day.

# Advisory: Third-party breach

## Situation

A Third-party breach situation has been reported or detected.

## Key Considerations

Vendor-side breaches activate the incident playbook and contractual notification timelines.

## Immediate Steps

1. Preserve all evidence in its current state.

2. Inform the owning team and the compliance or legal desk.

3. Do not communicate externally without approval.

## Escalation

Escalate to the relevant officer within the same business day.

# Know Your Customer (KYC) Standard

## Purpose
This standard defines the mandatory identity and address verification a
banker must perform before onboarding any customer, in line with the RBI
Master Direction on KYC.

## Acceptable Officially Valid Documents (OVDs)
- Aadhaar (with masked number, per UIDAI guideline).
- PAN.
- Passport.
- Driving licence issued by an RTO.
- Voter ID issued by ECI.
- Job card issued by NREGA duly signed by the state government.

## Video KYC (V-CIP)
V-CIP is available for eligible savings account onboarding subject to:
- Genuine live video with the customer visible from mid-torso up.
- Random challenge (e.g., "Show your OVD now").
- Officer capturing at least 3 still frames during the call.
- Geo-tagging of the customer's location.

## Periodic Update Frequency
| Risk Category | Frequency |
|---|---|
| High risk | Every 2 years |
| Medium risk | Every 8 years |
| Low risk | Every 10 years |

## Deficient KYC
Accounts flagged with deficient KYC are moved to `PARTIAL FREEZE` after
two written reminders. All debits (excluding regulatory / statutory dues)
are blocked in this state.

# Advisory: Regulator notice

## Situation

A Regulator notice situation has been reported or detected.

## Key Considerations

Regulatory requisitions route through legal intake with strict internal deadlines.

## Immediate Steps

1. Preserve all evidence in its current state.

2. Inform the owning team and the compliance or legal desk.

3. Do not communicate externally without approval.

## Escalation

Escalate to the relevant officer within the same business day.

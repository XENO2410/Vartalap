# Advisory: Cross-border remittance query

## Situation

A Cross-border remittance query situation has been reported or detected.

## Key Considerations

Remittance queries need FEMA purpose codes and the authorised dealer process.

## Immediate Steps

1. Preserve all evidence in its current state.

2. Inform the owning team and the compliance or legal desk.

3. Do not communicate externally without approval.

## Escalation

Escalate to the relevant officer within the same business day.

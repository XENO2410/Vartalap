# Aha — Employee Rewards Programme

## What is Aha?
Aha is the employee-facing rewards and recognition platform. Points are
earned through business-aligned actions and can be redeemed for vouchers,
merchandise and experiences on the Aha portal.

## Earning Points
| Trigger | Points |
|---|---|
| Successful employee referral hired | 5,000 |
| Cross-sell win (per qualifying win) | 250 |
| Audit-clean month (branch or ops team) | 500 |
| CSAT topping the region | 1,500 |
| Certification pass (approved list) | 2,000 |

## Redemption
- Minimum redemption balance: 1,000 points.
- Points expire 24 months after being awarded.
- Redemption catalogue: Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra vouchers, curated experiences,
  branded merchandise.

## Anti-Abuse Controls
- Referrals that leave within 6 months are clawed back automatically.
- Fake cross-sell entries are flagged during quarterly audit and lead to
  point reversal and disciplinary action.

## Support
For Aha portal issues, raise a ticket via HResponse under `Aha > Portal`.
For point disputes, use `Aha > Point Reversal`.

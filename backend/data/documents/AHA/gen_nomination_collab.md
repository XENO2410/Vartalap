# Nomination Guide: Collaboration Award

## What It Recognises

cross-functional delivery with customer impact.

## How To Nominate

1. Open the Aha portal > Awards > Nominate.

2. Complete the nomination form with a clear narrative.

3. Obtain the required endorsements.

## Evaluation

A cross-functional panel scores on impact and collaboration.

## Outcome

Winners receive points and recognition at the next town hall.
